## 1. Liquidity Analysis

### Current Ratio: 2.62

**Formula**: Current Ratio = Total Current Assets / Total Current Liabilities

**Calculation**:
- Total Current Assets: $19,049 million (2024 Consolidated Balance Sheet)
- Total Current Liabilities: $7,281 million (2024 Consolidated Balance Sheet)
- Current Ratio = 19,049 / 7,281 ≈ 2.62

**Interpretation**:  
AMD's current ratio of approximately 2.62 indicates a strong short-term liquidity position, with the company having $2.62 in current assets for every $1.00 of current liabilities. This suggests AMD can comfortably meet its short-term financial obligations.

### Quick Ratio: 1.83

**Formula**: Quick Ratio = (Current Assets - Inventories) / Current Liabilities

**Calculation**:
- Current Assets: $19,049 million
- Inventories: $5,734 million (2024 Consolidated Balance Sheet)
- Current Liabilities: $7,281 million
- Quick Ratio = (19,049 - 5,734) / 7,281 ≈ 1.83

**Interpretation**:  
The quick ratio of approximately 1.83 indicates solid liquidity excluding less liquid inventory assets, confirming AMD's ability to cover immediate liabilities.

### Liquidity Assessment

AMD exhibits a strong liquidity profile with both current and quick ratios well above 1.5, reflecting financial flexibility and short-term solvency as of the latest reporting period.
