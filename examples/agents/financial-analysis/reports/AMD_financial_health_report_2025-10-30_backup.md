## 1. Liquidity Analysis

### Current Ratio: 2.62

**Formula:** Current Ratio = Total Current Assets / Total Current Liabilities

**Calculation:**  
- Total Current Assets: $19,049 million (AMD Consolidated Balance Sheet, Page 58)  
- Total Current Liabilities: $7,281 million (AMD Consolidated Balance Sheet, Page 58)  
- Current Ratio = $19,049M / $7,281M = 2.62

**Interpretation:**  
AMD has a strong short-term liquidity position with a current ratio of 2.62, meaning it holds $2.62 in current assets for each $1.00 of current liabilities. This surpasses the typical benchmark of 1.0, indicating comfortable coverage of short-term obligations.

### Quick Ratio: 1.57

**Formula:** Quick Ratio = (Total Current Assets - Inventories - Prepaid Expenses and Other Current Assets) / Total Current Liabilities

**Calculation:**  
- Total Current Assets: $19,049 million  
- Inventories: $5,734 million (Page 58)  
- Prepaid Expenses and Other Current Assets: $1,878 million (Page 68)  
- Total Current Liabilities: $7,281 million  
- Quick Assets = $19,049M - $5,734M - $1,878M = $11,437M  
- Quick Ratio = $11,437M / $7,281M = 1.57

**Interpretation:**  
With a quick ratio of 1.57, AMD demonstrates its ability to cover all short-term obligations with liquid assets excluding less liquid inventories and prepaid expenses, signaling sound liquidity health.

### Liquidity Assessment

AMD’s liquidity ratios indicate a robust short-term financial position, providing ample liquidity buffers to meet liabilities without stress. This liquidity strength supports operational stability and financial flexibility in dynamic market conditions.

## 2. Profitability Analysis

### Margin Analysis

- **Gross Margin**: 49.3%  
  - Formula: Gross Margin = (Gross Profit / Net Revenue) × 100%  
  - Calculation: ($12,725M / $25,785M) × 100% = 49.3%  
  - Source: AMD Income Statement (AMD-AR.md, Pages 53-58)

- **Operating Margin**: 7.37%  
  - Formula: Operating Margin = (Operating Income / Net Revenue) × 100%  
  - Calculation: ($1,900M / $25,785M) × 100% = 7.37%  
  - Source: AMD Income Statement (AMD-AR.md, Pages 53-58)

- **Net Margin**: 6.36%  
  - Formula: Net Margin = (Net Income / Net Revenue) × 100%  
  - Calculation: ($1,641M / $25,785M) × 100% = 6.36%  
  - Source: AMD Income Statement (AMD-AR.md, Pages 53-58)

### Return Ratios

- **Return on Assets (ROA)**: 2.37%  
  - Formula: ROA = Net Income / Total Assets  
  - Calculation: $1,641M / $69,226M = 2.37%  
  - Source: AMD Balance Sheet (AMD-AR.md, Pages 58)

- **Return on Equity (ROE)**: 2.85%  
  - Formula: ROE = Net Income / Total Equity  
  - Calculation: $1,641M / $57,568M = 2.85%  
  - Source: AMD Balance Sheet (AMD-AR.md, Pages 58)

### Profitability Assessment

AMD exhibits a strong gross margin of 49.3%, indicating efficient production and strong pricing power. However, operating and net margins of 7.37% and 6.36% respectively suggest moderate profitability after operational costs and taxes. Return ratios ROA (2.37%) and ROE (2.85%) are modest, impacted by recent strategic investments and amortization of acquisition-related intangibles. Overall, AMD maintains reasonable profitability and operational efficiency consistent with industry dynamics and growth investments.
